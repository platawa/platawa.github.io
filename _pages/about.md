Hi! I'm Paridhi, a graduate student at MIT pursuing my M.Eng. and B.S. in EECS (Electrical Engineering and Computer Science) with a focus on Computer Science and Molecular Biology, along with a minor in Economics. I'm passionate about applying AI and machine learning to advance biological and therapeutic discovery.

Currently, I'm working on my Master's thesis with the **Feng Zhang Lab** at the Broad Institute of MIT & Harvard, where I investigate bacterial antiviral defense systems and conduct computational studies on protein evolution. I also conduct research at the **Koch Institute for Integrative Cancer Research** with the Langer/Traverso Lab, focusing on integrating foundation models for molecular design and developing novel AI approaches for drug discovery.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **machine learning, computational biology, and therapeutic development**. I've had the opportunity to work on diverse projects ranging from single-cell perturbation prediction at Eli Lilly to DNA repair mechanisms at MD Anderson Cancer Center. Through my work, I aim to develop data-efficient, biologically-informed AI systems that can accelerate scientific discovery and improve human health.

Beyond research, I'm deeply committed to mentorship, teaching, and community service. I serve as a Teaching Assistant for Probability & Statistics, an Associate Advisor helping students navigate their academic journeys, and co-founded **EasyComm**, a startup developing speech technology for children with Down Syndrome (supported by MIT Sandbox Innovation Fund).

When I'm not in the lab or classroom, you can find me rowing on the Charles River, hiking, baking, or working on science communication through blogging and videography.
