Hi! I'm Paridhi, a graduate student at MIT pursuing my M.Eng. and B.S. in EECS with a focus on Computer Science and Molecular Biology. I'm passionate about applying AI and machine learning to advance biological and therapeutic discovery.

Currently, I'm working on my Master's thesis with the **Feng Zhang Lab** at the Broad Institute, investigating bacterial antiviral defense systems and protein evolution. I also conduct research at the **Koch Institute** with the Langer/Traverso Lab on foundation models for molecular design and AI-driven drug discovery.
