Email GitHub Google Scholar LinkedIn Twitter

Hi! I recently graduated with my M.Eng. and B.S. in CS & Molecular Biology from MIT, with a minor in Economics. I am interested in developing algorithms, models, and frameworks to better understand and engineer biological processes to improve human health.

I recently founded Illume, a health tech startup in the YC S26 batch. I also completed my Master’s with Dr. Feng Zhang at the Broad Institute, where I used mechanistic interpretability to investigate DNA LLMs for non-coding genome discovery. I have also previously researched with Dr. Giovanni Traverso and Dr. Robert Langer at the Koch Institute on unifying representations in foundation models, quality-aware tokenization, and property-constrained molecular design.

Previously, I have interned in the AI Division @ Eli Lilly & Company, the Genetics Department @ MD Anderson Cancer Center, and Dianox (AI-powered biotech startup in Denmark).

You can email me at platawa@mit.edu!

Projects
Conditional Generation from Few-shot Examples for Protein Design

Oct 2025 – Dec 2025 · MIT 6.S981: AI for Protein Biology · with Zhilei Bei and Weilang Luo

Few-shot generative framework that conditions a generative multiple sequence alignment (MSA) protein language model on a small set of example sequences to synthesize novel proteins exhibiting desired features.

A Pseudo-Time Analysis of Cellular Trajectories in Alzheimer's Disease using scRNA Sequencing

Nov 2024 – Dec 2024 · MIT 6.8701: Machine Learning for Computational Biology · with Jenny Prince and Selina Qian

Used immune cell-specific single-nucleus RNA-seq data to model temporal gene expression dynamics in Alzheimer's Disease, generating cell- and sample-level pseudotime trajectories correlated with clinical progression and identifying key marker genes and pathways linked to immune activation and AD severity.

[Paper] [Slides]
Decoding MIT's Digital Diary: Combining Sentiment, Topic Modeling, and an MIT Confessions-Powered ChatBot for Social Data Analysis

Nov 2024 – Dec 2024 · MIT 6.8611: Quantitative Methods for Natural Language Processing · with Anika Puri and Sejal Rathi

Analyzed over 61,000 posts from MIT Confessions (2013–2024) with NLP techniques to uncover evolving trends in student sentiment, wellness, and social dynamics, and created a RAG + LLM-based ChatBot that allowed for context-aware engagement with the data to inform institutional support and policy.

[Paper] [Poster]
Encoder-Decoder Model for Multimodal SMILES Latent Space Representation Property Prediction

Apr 2024 – May 2024 · MIT 6.8711: Generative AI for Biology (Computational Systems Biology: Deep Learning in the Life Sciences) · with Vincent Lin and Vanessa Xiao

Proposed a novel multimodal architecture using an autoencoder and fine-tuned ChemBERTa model to generate feature embeddings for SMILES and gene expression datasets to feed into a regressor for IC50 drug viability prediction.
