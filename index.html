Hi! I recently graduated with my M.Eng. and B.S. in CS & Molecular Biology from MIT, with a minor in Economics. I am interested in developing algorithms, models, and frameworks to better understand and engineer biological processes to improve human health.

I recently founded Illume, a health tech startup in the YC S26 batch. I also completed my Master’s with Dr. Feng Zhang at the Broad Institute, where I used mechanistic interpretability to investigate DNA LLMs for non-coding genome discovery. I have also previously researched with Dr. Giovanni Traverso and Dr. Robert Langer at the Koch Institute on unifying representations in foundation models, quality-aware tokenization, and property-constrained molecular design.

Previously, I have interned in the AI Division @ Eli Lilly & Company, the Genetics Department @ MD Anderson Cancer Center, and Dianox (AI-powered biotech startup in Denmark).

You can email me at platawa@mit.edu!

Research Experience
Feng Zhang Lab — Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard

Graduate & Undergraduate Researcher · Aug 2022 – May 2026

Studied bacterial antiviral defense systems across the wet lab and the terminal: characterized defense proteins through bacterial growth assays, PCR, and cloning, and ran paired computational and wet-lab mutagenesis on a DRT reverse transcriptase system to pin down the sequence regions its function depends on. Separately led an independent computational study mapping the evolutionary diversity of the L7-protein domain with FoldSeek, DALI, AlphaFold, iTOL, and embedding visualization. Supported as an Eric & Wendy Schmidt Center Research & Innovation Scholar.

Langer / Traverso Lab — MIT Koch Institute for Integrative Cancer Research

Undergraduate Researcher · Aug 2023 – May 2026

Worked on getting biological structure into general-purpose language models. Helped build a framework that injects knowledge from biology-specific foundation models (ESM, UniMol) into general LLMs through shared latent representations; co-developed a de novo molecular design algorithm that generates under physiologically relevant constraints and studied what happens when several properties are constrained at once; and contributed to QA-Token, a reinforcement-learning approach to quality-aware tokenization, including its evaluation in the NLP text domain.

Eli Lilly and Company — AI & Technology Division

AI Research Intern · May 2025 – Aug 2025

Built and deployed an end-to-end ML fine-tuning pipeline for in silico single-cell perturbation prediction, then presented the work as a poster, a technical talk, and a final presentation to an audience of 70+ stakeholders and senior VPs.

Bin Wang Lab — MD Anderson Cancer Center

Research Intern, CPRIT-CURE Summer Program · May 2024 – Aug 2024

Investigated DNA repair by the Abraxas–BRCA1 complex using western blotting, immunofluorescence, FISH, and microscopy, and set up a collaboration with the Genetics Department's Microscopy Core Director and the Imaris imaging team to analyze the results.

Used immune cell-specific single-nucleus RNA-seq data to model temporal gene expression dynamics in Alzheimer's Disease, generating cell- and sample-level pseudotime trajectories correlated with clinical progression and identifying key marker genes and pathways linked to immune activation and AD severity.
